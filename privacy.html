AI Assistant for Amazon

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Privacy-first · no tracking · no accounts

Summary

AI Assistant for Amazon is built to be private. In its default (Free) mode, none of your data ever leaves your browser. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no servers of our own.

What the extension does with data

  • On Amazon pages, AI Assistant for Amazon adds an on-page panel. When you open that panel, it reads the current product's title, price, rating, and visible reviews from the page you are already viewing in order to analyze them. This happens only on Amazon domains and only for the product page you are on. In Free mode the analysis runs locally on your device and is not transmitted anywhere.
  • Pasted text in the toolbar popup is analyzed locally on your device.
  • Pinned products (for the Compare feature) and history are stored only in your browser's local storage and never transmitted. Removing the extension clears them.

Optional "Pro" mode (off by default)

If you explicitly enable Pro mode and configure a backend URL in Settings, the product text is sent only to the backend URL you configure, to generate a deeper AI analysis. It is not sent to any other party, and we do not operate that backend for you.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us. AI Assistant for Amazon has no analytics or tracking and does not transmit your data to the developer or any third party in Free mode.

Permissions

  • storage — saves your settings, pinned products, and history on your own device.
  • Host access to Amazon domains — so the on-page panel can appear and read the product you're viewing. The extension runs only on Amazon domains and on no other website.
  • activeTab + scripting — used by the toolbar popup's manual "grab from tab" action.

AI Assistant for Amazon does not alter Amazon's content, place orders, or read pages on non-Amazon sites.

Data sharing & sale

We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties. We do not use your data for any purpose unrelated to the extension's single purpose, and never for creditworthiness or lending.

Affiliation

AI Assistant for Amazon is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Amazon. "Amazon" is a trademark of Amazon.com, Inc.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact the developer at the email listed on the Chrome Web Store listing.
